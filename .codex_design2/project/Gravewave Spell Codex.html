T101 · ABILITY

FireballLobbed projectile, splash on impact, lasting embers

Vermilion comet on an arc. The signature is the arc trail — a curved bezier of ember sprites that fades from gold at the head to black at the tail. On impact a vermilion ring shouts the radius, then a brief ash plume settles. Each spec rewrites the climax: Hydra spawns a head, Meteor reroutes the cast through the meteor system, Heat just makes it bigger and brighter.

T102 · ABILITY

A jagged peacock-blue fork instead of a smooth beam. Two-tone: an electric inner core and a warm-white halo so it reads even on the parchment background.
